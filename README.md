# StudentsAI

A study tool for university students, being rebuilt as a tutor that has read your course material.
You drop in your lecture slides, it quizzes you on them, grades your answers against the slides, and shows you what to study next.

**Status: under construction.**
The first version is retired and the rebuild is in progress.
The live site at [studentsai.org](https://www.studentsai.org) is a preview, and the app is not usable yet.

Built with Next.js, FastAPI, and PostgreSQL with pgvector, with the language model behind a provider-agnostic layer.
